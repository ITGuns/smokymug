## 1. Design board choices (marked in green on the option circles)

How to read the marks (verified against the stroke geometry and close-up zooms):
the client marks a chosen option by drawing a green X or cross ON its selection
circle (L1-A, L2-D, L4-A, L5-A, L6-A, Round, Standard), and in two spots used a
green swoosh checkmark instead (Full color, Bigger). The X's are selections, not
rejections: there is exactly one mark per option row, most sit on the "(current)"
option, green means keep per the board legend, and the NW01 sketch repeats the
L6-A month-grid layout. Red X's on the site panels are the opposite: those mean
remove (board legend "cross out = remove it").
